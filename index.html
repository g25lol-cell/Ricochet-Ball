Effective date: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the current version of Ricochet Ball (“the Game”) handles information when you play. It covers the Game itself. If you download it through an app store or visit a separately hosted policy page, that platform or website host may handle information under its own privacy terms.

Who we are

The Game is published by cdeveloper (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions, use the contact details in the Contact section.

Information and storage

The Game does not require a name, email address, account, or profile. Its current version does not transmit your score, gameplay activity, device identifiers, or other personal information to our servers.

Your best score is saved locally on your device using the device’s app storage. It is used only to show your personal record in the Game. We do not receive or keep a copy. The Game does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Local app data may be removed when you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it. Device backup and restore features are controlled by your operating system and settings.

Sharing and third-party services

The current version of the Game does not send personal information to us or share it with third parties. The Game has no account, social, advertising, or online leaderboard features.

You may obtain the Game from an app store. The store provider processes information under its own policies and is responsible for its store services. This policy does not describe those separate services.

Children’s privacy

The current version of the Game does not ask players to submit personal information and does not transmit gameplay data to us. If a future version adds features that collect personal information, we will update this policy and add any required notices and protections before those features are made available.

Your choices and rights

Your locally stored best score can be removed by clearing the Game’s app storage or uninstalling the Game, subject to your device’s backup settings. Because the current Game does not send personal information to us, we do not have a player account or gameplay profile to retrieve or delete from our servers.

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights relating to personal information. Contact us using the details below if you have a question or believe information connected with the Game has been sent to us. We will review and respond as applicable law requires.

Security and retention

The best score is stored in the Game’s local app storage and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not retain a server-side gameplay record. Protect access to your device using the controls provided by your operating system.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the Game’s features or data practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. Please review this page occasionally.
